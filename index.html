EWB-001 | E-Way Bill Not Generated

KEDB ID: DC451OBD12001

Category: E-Invoice / EWAY BILL

Process: Eway Bill

Issue Description: E WAY BILL NOT GENERATED FOR DOC NO 3702578875

System Error: 240 / Could not generate eway bill, pls contact helpdesk

Root Cause: The STN was created with 999 line items. As per process, only 250 HSN line items are allowed.

Resolution: Coordinate with Commercial Team to cancel the existing STN and create a new STN within 250 HSN line items.

Reach Out: Commercial Team
